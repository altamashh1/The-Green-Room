How it works

Three steps. One conversation.

No question banks to scroll through, no forms to fill out mid-interview. Pick a role, and the room does the rest.

01

Set the scene

Choose your target role, seniority level, and how many questions you want — a quick 3-question warm-up or a full 5-question round.

02

Talk it through

The interviewer asks a question out loud, you answer by speaking naturally, and it reacts — asking a real follow-up when your answer leaves something worth digging into, just like a real hiring manager would.

03

Get the debrief

Walk away with a score, a breakdown of every answer, and concrete notes on what to say differently next time — saved to your dashboard so you can track improvement round over round.

Why it feels different

Built to feel like a real interview, not a quiz.

Voice-driven, start to finish

Questions are read aloud, you answer out loud — no typing under pressure. Just like the real thing.

Adaptive follow-ups

The interviewer listens to what you actually say and probes deeper when it matters, instead of reading a fixed script.

Structured, honest feedback

Every answer gets a strength and a specific thing to sharpen — not vague encouragement, actual notes you can use.

Timed, realistic rounds

A time budget per round keeps you thinking on your feet instead of over-rehearsing every word.

Progress you can see

Every round is saved to your dashboard, so you can see your score trend up as you practice more.

Practice on your schedule

No booking a slot with a friend or coach — start a round whenever you have five spare minutes.
